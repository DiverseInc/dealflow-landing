No. The app runs entirely in the browser. Data persists in localStorage. To share data across a team, deploy to any static host (Vercel, Netlify, etc.) and everyone uses the same URL.

Can I customize the branding?

Does it work for mortgage brokers too?

Yes. Toggle between Real Estate, Mortgage, or All mode. Mortgage mode includes loan pipeline, borrower CRM, loan officer scorecards, mortgage KPIs, and 5 mortgage-specific workflows.

Email support is included with every license. We'll help you get the app running on your domain and customize it to your brand.
